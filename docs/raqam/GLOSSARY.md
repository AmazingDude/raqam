# Raqam

Raqam is edge runners' accounting app, with bookkeeping as its primary purpose and reconciliation as a supporting function.

## Language

**Bookkeeping**:
Recording and organizing a business's financial activity.

**Reconciliation**:
Comparing financial records and supporting evidence to identify agreement, differences and unresolved relationships.

**Raqam**:
The current Financial Operations hackathon product. Earlier ShiftProof vocabulary belongs to a different candidate.

**Cashbook**:
The business's recorded cash inflows and outflows, together with their reviewed categories and supporting evidence. It is distinct from a complete double-entry ledger.

**Accrual basis**:
Recording income when it is earned and expenses when they are incurred, rather than only when cash is received or paid.

**Chart of accounts**:
The organized list of financial accounts used to classify and summarize a business's assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses.

**Double-entry ledger**:
The record of approved journal entries and account balances. Each journal entry has debit and credit lines whose totals balance. It includes non-cash receivables/payables as well as cash activity.

**Journal entry**:
A dated, reviewed financial event represented by balanced debit and credit lines. A source document or AI proposal alone is not an approved journal entry.

**Accounts receivable**:
Amounts customers owe the business for goods or services already earned but not yet collected.

**Accounts payable**:
Amounts the business owes suppliers for goods or services already received but not yet paid.

**Opening balance**:
The balance of an account at the start of an accounting period.

**Transaction**:
A recorded cash inflow or outflow. A supporting receipt is evidence for a movement, not a second cash movement.

**Supporting document**:
A receipt, supplier bill, customer invoice or other record supplied as financial evidence. Its presence alone does not establish that payment settled.

**Category proposal**:
An AI-suggested cashbook label awaiting the owner's review. It is distinct from an accepted category.

**Match proposal**:
A suggested relationship between a transaction and a supporting document. It remains unresolved until reviewed.

**Accepted match**:
A user-confirmed relationship between one transaction and one supporting document.

**Review decision**:
The owner's acceptance, correction or rejection of a proposed financial record or relationship.

**Cash movement**:
Cash inflows less cash outflows over the selected records and period. It is not profit.

**Financial statement**:
A report that summarizes financial activity or balances for a stated period or as-of date.

**Unresolved record**:
A record requiring clarification, correction, categorization or supporting evidence. It is not a fraud finding.

**Source reference**:
A locator for the original supplied row or document supporting a record, proposal or answer.

**Workspace**:
One owner's business records and supporting evidence. It is separate from other owners' workspaces.
