# Raqam artwork

- `raqam-logo.png`: original user-supplied artwork, preserved unchanged.
- `raqam-mark.png`: R-only transparent cutout, used by `Mark`, `Logo`, browser and Apple icons.

Created using the built-in imagegen tool with this prompt:

> Edit the supplied Raqam logo into an exact transparent cutout of ONLY the upper decorative R emblem. Remove the entire RAQAM text wordmark beneath it. Preserve the upper R emblem's exact silhouette, mosque-like curved top, gold diamond ornaments, gold bars, dark emerald green colors, gold trim, texture and geometric mosaic details. Remove the white background completely, including all white negative spaces inside the R and the white gaps separating the mosaic pieces, with clean antialiased edges and no white halo, no drop shadow, no white stroke. Do not redesign, simplify, recolor, or add anything. Center the isolated R emblem on a transparent square canvas with a small even margin. Must be readable against black as well as light backgrounds. Output PNG with actual alpha transparency.

Validated alpha transparency in the asset and generated 192px icons through CLI pixel checks. No GUI inspection was performed.
